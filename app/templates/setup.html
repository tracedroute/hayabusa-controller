Configure GitOps

Advanced mode uses GitHub/GitLab as the source of truth. Admins link a Git instance to a controller team. People may only publish playbooks they own (or their team owns), and only after signing in with a matching GitHub/GitLab account. Site-wide Core↔controller jobs (Approve + lan.relay) are unchanged.
